# 🗺️ Roadmap

*Early testing. Plans can change, and there are no dates yet. Suggestions welcome!*

## ✅ Done
- **Level cap 99, subjob 99** (99/99)
- **EXP x2.5**, **drops x2**, **gil drops x2**, **movement speed +50%**
- **Skill-ups at the maximum rate** for every skill: combat, magic, crafting, fishing and digging
- **Lottery NMs always pop** when their placeholder is killed, with no respawn timer
- **Every monster drops gil** (at least 500)
- **Starter kit:** 100,000 gil, all maps, all outpost warps, all Survival Guides
- **Commands:** `!buff` (10 hours), `!home`, `!city`, `!tele` / `!telelist`, `!shop`, `!status` (stats of your target or yourself)
- Server open for small-group testing

## 🧪 Live, in testing
- **The Augmenter** (Lower Jeuno): add bonus stats to your gear. Item stats stay retail, and augments are how you get stronger. It's brand new, so tell us if anything looks off.

## 🔨 Now
- **Bug hunting:** playing through the core systems and fixing what breaks
- **Tuning the Augmenter:** prices and bonuses, based on your feedback
- **`!ah`:** Auction House from anywhere (needs a small server change, being looked at)
- **More quality-of-life commands** (ideas: `!mh`, `!escape`, `!signet`, `!gotoexp`)

## 📋 Planned (rough order)
1. **Daily hunts:** daily tasks paying EXP, gil and gear currency
2. **Daily BCNMs** from level 20, with level-appropriate rare / HQ gear
3. **Gear progression** built on the augments
4. **Adventure Quest:** a custom questline
5. **Small-group raid:** a custom Dynamis-style endgame event
6. **Synergy crafting:** custom recipes

## 💭 Ideas (no promises)
- A stocked Auction House
- Seasonal events and a monthly bonus day
- Custom relic / empyrean progression

Post what you were doing, where, and roughly when. It helps a lot.
